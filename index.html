About NUM

A Legacy of Excellence in Management Education

The National University of Management stands as Cambodia's foremost public institution dedicated to business, economics, and management education. Founded in 1968, NUM has evolved into a dynamic academic powerhouse that blends decades of institutional wisdom with a forward-looking commitment to innovation and global relevance. Our mission is clear: to produce graduates who are not only academically accomplished but also ethically grounded and industry-ready.

At the core of NUM's academic identity are four strategic pillars — Management, Economics, Information & Communication Technology, and Law. These disciplines are carefully integrated to reflect the interconnected nature of today's professional landscape, ensuring that every student develops a multidisciplinary perspective that sets them apart in an increasingly competitive global economy.

As a premier public university, NUM takes pride in its role as a national catalyst for socioeconomic development. Through rigorous research, strategic international partnerships, and deep engagement with Cambodia's public and private sectors, the National University of Management continues to shape the policies, enterprises, and institutions that define the nation's future.

Academic Excellence

Programs Designed for Tomorrow's Leaders

Our curriculum is continuously refined in partnership with global industry leaders to ensure relevance, rigor, and real-world impact.

Global Business

Prepares students for global roles through international exchange programs in Europe and Asia, focusing on economics, trade, and legal systems. Focuses on startups, digital economy, and managing, developing, and leading innovative ventures, according to the NUM International College Booklet.

  • Dual-degree opportunities with partner universities across Asia, Europe, and North America
  • Curriculum aligned with AACSB international accreditation standards
  • Mandatory international internship placement in Year 3

Entrepreneurship & Innovation

The NUM International College offers an English-based BBA focusing on creating innovative business models, new products, and improving organizational systems. Social Innovation Lab serves as an incubator and maker space equipped with 3D printers and virtual reality tools.

  • On-campus startup incubator with seed funding access
  • Mentorship from Cambodia's top entrepreneurs and investors
  • Pitch competitions with regional and international exposure

Digital Economy & ICT

The Faculty of Digital Economy (FDE) at the National University of Management (NUM) offers specialized English-based programs, including Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Digital Economy, Financial Technology (FinTech), and Smart City Planning Management. A four-year program focusing on digital economic practices, new technology impacts, and business digitalization. Prepares students for roles in digital payments, AI, and blockchain-driven finance. Focuses on designing sustainable, tech-driven urban solutions and targets professionals aiming to specialize in digital solutions for business

  • Hands-on training in Python, cloud computing, and machine learning
  • Industry projects with leading tech firms operating in Cambodia
  • Focus on fintech, e-commerce, and digital government solutions

Economics & Public Policy

Focuses on digital technologies, business innovation, fintech, and smart city management, aimed at professionals in both public and private sectors. Designed to train leaders in developing, implementing, and evaluating government policy, with emphasis on economic and social challenges. Integrates economics, law, sociology, and political science within the context of digitalization.

  • Research partnerships with the Ministry of Economy and Finance
  • Econometric modeling and data-driven policy analysis training
  • Publication opportunities in ASEAN economic journals

Business Law & Governance

This program focus on the legal frameworks governing corporate entities, commercial transactions, and governance structures within Cambodia and in a globalized context. The MBL focuses on corporate governance, regulation, structuring, financing, and management. It covers the legal frameworks governing businesses from formation to dissolution. This program is designed to provide thorough, advanced knowledge of legislative issues crucial to companies and regulatory bodies.

  • Comparative law approach covering ASEAN and international trade law
  • Moot court competitions and legal clinic experience
  • Focus on ESG compliance and corporate governance standards

Finance & Accounting

This programs offer a comprehensive 4-year undergraduate degree designed to prepare students for corporate finance, banking, and auditing roles. It integrates local Cambodian accounting standards with international practices, focusing on financial management, taxation, and investment.

  • ACCA and CPA-aligned curriculum pathways
  • Bloomberg Terminal access and financial simulation labs
  • Direct pipeline to Cambodia's banking and financial sector

Student Life

A Vibrant Community Where Ideas Thrive

Life at the National University of Management extends far beyond the lecture hall. Our campus in the heart of Phnom Penh pulses with energy — from student-led business case competitions and TED-style talks to cultural festivals that celebrate Cambodia's rich heritage alongside global perspectives. With over 30 active student clubs and organizations, there is a place for every passion.

NUM students benefit from a thriving ecosystem that bridges academic learning with real-world experience. Regular industry networking events, guest lectures by C-suite executives, and field visits to Cambodia's leading enterprises ensure that learning is always connected to practice. Our annual Innovation Week and Entrepreneurship Summit have become flagship events on the national calendar.

International exchange is woven into the fabric of student life. Each year, hundreds of NUM students study abroad through partnerships with universities in Japan, South Korea, France, Australia, and across Southeast Asia — while international students from over 20 countries choose NUM as their gateway to understanding Cambodia's dynamic economy.
